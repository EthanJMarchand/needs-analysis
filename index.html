Tax and probate on death

Canada has no inheritance tax. Probate is a court fee on what actually goes through the estate. A non-registered account, and other assets that move through the estate, can be taxed as a capital gain on death. An RRSP is income on the final return unless it rolls to an eligible beneficiary. Those taxes are added to the life insurance need, and the chart raises them as the accounts and the estate value grow. This is a screen, not a tax opinion.
